`start_element_event_handler2` and `end_element_event_handler2` pass `XML_GetCurrentColumnNumber`/`XML_GetCurrentLineNumber` (type `XML_Size`, unsigned long) to the `int` parameters of `StructData_AddItem`. In `external_entity_duff_loader`, the `unsigned int` loop counter is assigned to the `int g_allocation_count`. In these tests the line and column numbers and the loop bound (10) are small, so I made each conversion explicit with an `(int)` cast. Behaviour is unchanged.
