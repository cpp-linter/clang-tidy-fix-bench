At `END:VEVENT`, `event` and `exdates` are moved into `parsed`, but they stay alive across loop iterations. clang-tidy cannot tell that a later iteration would reset them at `BEGIN:VEVENT` before using them again. Right after the `push_back`, they are now reset with `event = CalendarEvent{}` and `exdates.clear()`, so they are always in a known, valid state after the move. Parsing results are unchanged, because the next `BEGIN:VEVENT` resets them the same way.
