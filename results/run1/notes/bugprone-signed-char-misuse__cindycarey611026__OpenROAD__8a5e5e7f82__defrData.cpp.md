In `upperCase`, `int i = (int)*place` indexed the 256-entry `defiShift` table. A byte >= 0x80 produced a negative index, which reads out of bounds. That is a real bug, fixed by converting through `static_cast<unsigned char>`. In `validateMaskShiftInput`, `curShift` is likewise taken as `static_cast<unsigned char>(shiftMask[i])`. The '0'..'9' range check behaves the same, since high bytes are still rejected.
