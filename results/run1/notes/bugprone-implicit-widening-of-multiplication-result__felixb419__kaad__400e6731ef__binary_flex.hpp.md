`value.shape()[i] * this->value_stride[i]` multiplied two `int`s and then stored the result in a `std::size_t` element of `C_offset`. Casting the shape to `std::size_t` first performs the product in the destination type, which avoids `int` overflow for large tensors. Under modular arithmetic the result is unchanged for every value that did not overflow before, including negative strides.
