The edge-button weighting `X = X * (1 + DEF_SENSOR_EDGE_WEIGHT);` computes a `double` (the weight is a floating constant) and stores it back into the `uint16_t X` implicitly. I made the truncation explicit with `X = (uint16_t)(X * (1 + DEF_SENSOR_EDGE_WEIGHT));`. Behaviour is the same: the value is truncated toward zero as before, and the very next statement saturates X to at most 50.
