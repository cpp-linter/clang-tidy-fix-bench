In the dropdown logic, the first branch of the if/else-if chain (cancel on an open, focused list) and the last branch (pointer released outside the items) had the same body, `st->flag = false;`. The two branches are not adjacent and their order matters (the cancel must win over a field click, and `take_activate()` must not be consumed after a cancel), so I evaluate the cancel once into `const bool cancelled` and fold it into the last branch's condition (`cancelled || ...`), while guarding the click and activate branches with `!cancelled`. Short-circuiting keeps the same calls in the same order, so behaviour is unchanged.
