The `least_uint` partial specializations compared `N` with the literal bounds `0xffU`, `0xffffU` and `0xffff'ffffU`. Those values are just the maxima of `uint8_t`, `uint16_t` and `uint32_t`. I replaced them with `UINT8_MAX`, `UINT16_MAX` and `UINT32_MAX` from `<cstdint>`, which the file already includes, so no new include is needed. The values and the selected types do not change.
